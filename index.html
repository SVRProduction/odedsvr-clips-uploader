OdedSVR Clips Uploader

An internal desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel OdedSVR Clips on YouTube.

It edits clips from the owner's own Kick livestreams and uses the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) to upload them to his own YouTube channel and schedule their publication: videos.insert (private, with a scheduled publish time), thumbnails.set, and channels.list (to confirm the signed-in channel is OdedSVR Clips before any upload).

The tool is not publicly available, has no other users, does not read or modify any other channel's data, and does not collect data about anyone.
